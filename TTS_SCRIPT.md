# Bharat Darshan — Walkthrough Video TTS Script (2–3 minutes)

## [0:00 – 0:15] INTRO
"Welcome to Bharat Darshan — Heritage Atlas India. Twenty-five monuments, one atlas of India. Built for Blithchron 2027 at IIT Gandhinagar, Track 2. This is a geospatial, bilingual, source-cited heritage archive — not a static report. Every fact comes from a verified source. Nothing is invented."

## [0:15 – 0:35] LANDING PAGE
"Here's the landing page. A full-screen rotating photograph of one of the monuments. At the bottom left, the 'Enter the archive' button takes you into the archive. The navigation bar has ARCHIVE, STORIES, TIMELINE, MAP, JOURNEY, QUIZ, FOLKLORE, ABOUT, and HOW IT WAS MADE. The language toggle switches between English and Hindi — EN and हिं — and it applies to both the content and the chrome."

## [0:35 – 0:55] HERITAGE MAP
"Let's open the Heritage Map. Twenty-five monuments as teardrop pins, color-coded by type — tomb, temple, cave, fort, and more. You can filter by region and type. Scroll to zoom. Click any pin and a preview card opens with a photograph, UNESCO and region tags, a bilingual title, a quick fact, and three buttons: Open the record, Add to Journey, and Mark Visited. The journey route appears as a dashed ochre polyline with numbered pins connecting your selected stops."

## [0:55 – 1:10] FOLKLORE
"Now the Folklore page. Every site has a verified folklore note — 640 to 960 words — covering local legends and nearby attractions. Each entry cites Wikipedia. Click 'Read full folklore' to open a dedicated page with the full legend and nearby attractions in long-form prose. No folklore is invented. Where a legend could not be verified, the field stays empty."

## [1:10 – 1:25] TIMELINE
"The Timeline shows all 25 sites in chronological order, grouped into four era bands: Ancient, Medieval, Mughal, and Colonial. It shows every site regardless of filter — no hiding. Honest date rules: 20 TODOs stay TODO. Nothing is filled in."

## [1:25 – 1:40] STORIES
"Stories presents the same writing as the site page but as long-form articles. Each monument has a five-stage narrative — Why it was built, Who built it, What to notice, How it changed over time, and Why it matters today. The standfirst is the site's own description, set larger. Hindi translations sit beside the English."

## [1:40 – 1:55] JOURNEY & QUIZ
"The Journey page lets you build a route of up to five stops. You see how far apart they are, and the route appears on the map. The Quiz has 75 questions — three per site — with score tracking. All bilingual."

## [1:55 – 2:10] SITE PAGE
"Open any site — say, the Taj Mahal. You get the full record: quick facts, the five-stage story, history, architecture, why it matters, folklore and nearby attractions, sources, and photo credits. Every sentence comes from the data file. No paraphrasing."

## [2:10 – 2:25] DATA HONESTY
"Data honesty is our core principle. Twenty TODOs stay TODO. Twenty-five folklore notes were null — now sourced from Wikipedia. One hundred twenty-five story images stay null. Forty-seven sources, every one verified as real. One hundred twenty-seven quick facts. No invented dates, dimensions, quotes, or UNESCO labels."

## [2:25 – 2:40] TECHNICAL & CLOSE
"Built with React 18, TypeScript, Vite, Leaflet, and Zustand. No react-router — a hand-rolled 60-line hash router. The build passes in under three seconds. The palette is soft stone, ochre as the sole accent, terracotta, rust, steel blue, and ink navy. Two accents per panel maximum."

## [2:40 – 2:50] OUTRO
"Bharat Darshan — Heritage Atlas India. Twenty-five monuments, one atlas. Built by team kairo aeon tech, Team ID 5D6656351076. Riddhi Agrawal, Aarya Sharma, Ishan Parmar. For Blithchron 27, IIT Gandhinagar. Thank you."
