The Problem

India's heritage is scattered, flat, disconnected.

No single place lets a student, tourist, or researcher explore monuments spatially, read verified folklore, walk a timeline, and track their own journey.

1. Scattered

Monuments documented in PDFs, ASI reports, and tourism pages. No unified interface.

2. Static

Maps without context. Timelines without navigation. No spatial exploration.

3. Disconnected

Folklore, history, and architecture live in separate silos. No cross-referencing.

4. Fragile

Folklore often fabricated. No source citation. No provenance for claims.

The gap: location-based, interactive mapping with folklore and architectural discovery — exactly our chosen pathway.

The Solution

Explore. Learn. Preserve.

Bharat Darshan — a geospatial, bilingual, source-cited heritage archive. Not a static report.

🗺️ Heritage Map

25 monuments as teardrop pins on Leaflet. Region + type filters. Click any pin → photo, facts, actions.

📜 Folklore & Nearby

640–960 word verified folklore notes per site. Local legends + nearby attractions. Each cites Wikipedia.

⏱️ Timeline

Four eras. All 25 sites. Chronological. Honest date rules — TODO stays TODO.

📖 Stories

Five-stage narrative per monument. Standfirst, stages, Hindi translation. Same text everywhere.

🚶 Journey Route

Mark visited. Add to journey. Dashed polyline with numbered pins. Distance between stops.

🧠 Quiz

75 questions. 3 per site. Score tracking. Bilingual. Drawn from the archive.
